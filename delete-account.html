Weekbu Account & Data Deletion

Request Account Deletion

To request deletion of your Weekbu account and associated authentication data, please send an email to: CSA_@outlook.de Subject: Delete Weekbu Account Your Firebase Authentication account will then be permanently deleted.

What Data Gets Deleted

The following data will be deleted: • Firebase Authentication account • Email address • Authentication identifiers Budget and financial data is stored locally on your device and is not stored on external servers. Removing the app from your device deletes locally stored budgeting data.

Data Retention

Account deletion requests are processed within 30 days. Some data may be temporarily retained if legally required.
