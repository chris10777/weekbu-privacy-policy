Contact Support

Need help with Weekbu? You can contact us directly via email: CSA_@outlook.de We usually respond within 48 hours.

Restore Purchases

If you purchased Weekbu Pro and your premium access is not active: • Open Settings • Tap "Restore Purchases" Your subscription or lifetime purchase will be restored automatically.

Account Deletion

To request account deletion, please visit: Delete Account Your authentication account and associated account information can be permanently removed upon request.

About Weekbu

Weekbu is a minimalist weekly budgeting app designed to help users track expenses, stay within budget, and build better spending habits.
